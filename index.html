To My Ma, From My Heart 🤍🥺 My dearest Ma, I don’t even know how to start this letter because my heart is full of regret for what I did to you. 😭🙏🏻 Ma, I’m truly sorry. I never wanted to hurt you or make you feel like I don’t trust you. The pain from our past fight suddenly came back into my mind, and I let my fear and overthinking control me. Instead of calming myself down and talking to you, I made the wrong decision. I know my feelings don’t justify what I did, and I’m not writing this to prove myself right. Ma, you always tell me to trust you first, and now I understand why those words are so important. You deserve to feel loved, respected and trusted by the person who wants to spend his whole life with you. I don’t want to become someone who makes you feel like you always have to prove yourself to me. I know your past doesn’t define the person you are today. I know you have your own dreams, your own life and your own future. I should respect you as the person you are now, instead of allowing my fears to make decisions for me. Ma, I want to build our future together. I’m working hard because I want us to have a better life, and I want to see you happy beside me. But what is the point of building a beautiful future if I don’t learn how to protect the peace and trust between us? I need to work on my heart and mind too, not just our future. I promise I’ll learn to trust you, Ma. When my overthinking comes back, I’ll learn to calm myself down and communicate with you instead of acting on my fears. I’ll respect your privacy, your feelings and your space. I want my actions to show you that I’m learning from this mistake. I also know that you don’t have to forgive me immediately. I won’t force you to trust me just because I’m apologising. I understand that trust is something I have to respect and earn through my actions. Sometimes I hate how my own mind can turn my fears into something that hurts the person I love the most. But I don’t want to keep repeating the same mistake. I want to become a better man, a better partner and one day, a better husband to you. I pray to Bhagavan Krishna to help me control my fears, give me a peaceful mind and teach me how to love you in the right way. 🙏🏻🤍 Ma, I’m sorry for the pain I caused you. I’m sorry for making you question whether I truly trust you. You mean so much more to me than my fears, and I don’t want my overthinking to keep hurting our relationship. You don’t have to reply to this letter right away. Take your time and have the space you need. I just wanted you to know what’s in my heart. I’m sorry, Ma. From the bottom of my heart. 😭🙏🏻🤍 With all my love, Your Indran 🤍
